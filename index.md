      <h1>Biography</h1>
      
      <p class="text-muted">
        I am an assistant researcher at <u>Tsinghua University</u> (2023-), working with
        Prof. <a href="https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=7t2jzpgAAAAJ&hl=en">Wenwu Zhu </a>
        and 
        Prof. <a href = "https://mn.cs.tsinghua.edu.cn/xinwang/">Xin Wang</a>
        at the Department of Computer Science and Technology.
        I am a visiting student at <u>Simon Fraser University</u> (2021.7-2022.7), working with
        Prof. <a href="https://www.cs.sfu.ca/~jcliu/index.htm">Jiangchuan Liu</a>
        at the School of Computer Science.
        I received my Ph.D. from <u>Beijing University of Posts and Telecommunications</u> (2018-2023), supervised by
        Prof. <a href="https://www.ebupt.com/list/roll/snavid/7">Jianxin Liao</a>,
        Prof. <a href="https://teacher.bupt.edu.cn/wangjingyu/zh_CN/index.htm">Jingyu Wang</a>
        and
        Prof. <a href="https://teacher.bupt.edu.cn/qiqi">Qi Qi</a>
        at the State Key Laboratory of Networking and Switching Technology, School of Computer and Science.
        My recent research interests include <b>Embodied AI, World Model, and Multimedia Intelligence</b>.
